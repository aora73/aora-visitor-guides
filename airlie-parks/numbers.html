How the numbers were made

  • Town centre is the Airlie Beach Lagoon, Shute Harbour Road (−20.2672, 148.7174). Drive times and distances are one-way car routes on the OpenStreetMap road network (OSRM), free-flow, not live traffic. Checked 24 November 2026.
  • Where a park’s own copy says a shorter time — Conway “about 30 minutes”, Hydeaway “about 45”, Proserpine “about 20” — that line is quoted on the card. The table uses the mapped route, which is the slower, longer figure.
  • Google scores are from Google’s hotel listings (or, for Tasman and Conway, a published extract of those reviews). Tripadvisor scores are from each property page. Hydeaway’s Google 3.9 is stable across directories; the review count is about 200 on Google-style listings and higher when mixed sources are bundled, so the count is shown as approximate. Proserpine did not have a separate Google total in the sources checked.
  • Scores move. Facilities, pet rules and construction notes were taken from operator pages current in October–November 2026 (Camplify’s 13 October 2026 roundup, BIG4, NRMA, Discovery, Tasman, Whitsunday Regional Council, Hydeaway Bay). Ring the park before you rely on pets, a dump point marked “Ask”, or a site under the water slide.
  • Photographs are real and freely licensed, mostly from Wikimedia Commons. Where a current photo of the grounds was not available, the picture is of the beach or the town, and the caption says so. None of them are generated.
